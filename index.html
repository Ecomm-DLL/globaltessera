Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Tessera Global LLC handles personal information when you visit this site, contact us or buy through our online store.

Information we receive

When you email us, we receive your name, email address and the information you choose to include. The contact form on this site prepares a draft in your own email app. We receive that message only when you send it. Please leave payment details out of support messages.

When you shop

Depending on the order, we and our service providers use your contact details, billing and shipping information, items purchased, payment status and order history. Shipping carriers may also provide delivery updates. Storefront checkout notices explain any information specific to that store.

How we use information

We use this information to process purchases, ship your purchases, respond to questions, manage returns, prevent fraud and keep records required by law. Where applicable, we rely on performance of our contract with you, legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and securing our store, or consent where required.

Service providers and international processing

We share the information needed for hosting, payments, fulfillment, delivery and customer support with the providers involved in those tasks. We may also disclose information to meet legal obligations. Providers may process information in the United States or other countries, subject to safeguards required by applicable law.

Site technology

This institutional site does not set advertising cookies or use advertising pixels. Hosting infrastructure may process connection details, such as an IP address and request information, to serve and secure the site. Our store explains its checkout cookies and preferences where applicable.

Retention and your choices

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the order, address support requests and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy of your information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it.

Contact and requests

Email support@globaltessera.com to reach Tessera Global LLC about privacy. Tell us which purchase or communication your request concerns. We may ask for information to verify your identity. Where applicable, you may contact your local data protection authority.

Refund Policy.

You may request a refund for a physical product within 30 calendar days after you receive it. If items arrive separately, the window begins when you receive each item.

How to request a return

Email support@globaltessera.com within the 30-day window. Include your order number or purchase email and the item you would like to return. Sending the request within this window starts the process.

Return instructions and costs

We will provide the appropriate return address, instructions and any applicable shipping arrangements before you send the item. Please wait for these instructions so your return can be matched to your order. Any return cost charged to you must have been disclosed before purchase and be permitted by applicable law.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

If your order arrives damaged or differs from what you purchased, describe the issue when you contact us. A photo can help us identify the problem. If the parcel has not arrived, we will check the shipment and discuss the available remedy. The 30-day window for a received product does not start while it is in transit.

Receiving your refund

We return approved refunds to the original payment method. We will confirm when the refund has been issued. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your payment provider. Any refund deadlines and additional remedies required by applicable law apply.

Your consumer rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under applicable consumer law, including rights relating to faulty goods and cancellations.

Shipping & Delivery.

Physical products generally arrive in 10–20 calendar days from order confirmation.

Physical delivery

The 10–20 day window is an estimate for supported destinations. The delivery estimate and any shipping charges specific to your order are shown at checkout. Timing can vary with the destination, carrier and customs clearance.

Where we deliver

We serve customers worldwide where fulfillment is available and the transaction is permitted. Available destinations depend on the product, carrier coverage and applicable trade restrictions. Check your shipping address at the storefront before placing an order.

Delays and tracking

Check your order email for shipment details or a tracking link when one is available. If we cannot ship within the time promised, we will contact you to offer a revised shipping date or cancellation with a refund for the unshipped item. If an order is overdue, contact us with the order number so we can investigate.

After delivery

For a physical product, the 30-day refund request window begins on receipt. Read the Refund Policy for instructions.
