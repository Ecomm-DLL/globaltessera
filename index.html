Privacy Policy.

Effective November 18, 2026

Tessera Global LLC is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. It applies when you use this company website, contact our team or purchase men’s goods through our own online stores.

What we collect and where it comes from

You provide contact details when you ask a question, such as your name, email address, phone number if supplied, and the contents of your message. When you order, we receive the contact, billing and delivery details needed for the purchase, along with the items ordered, order number, amount and payment status. Our payment, fulfillment and carrier partners may provide transaction confirmations, tracking events, delivery confirmations or return updates.

How the contact form works

The form on this website prepares a draft in your email app. The name, email address and message you enter are used in your browser to prepare that draft. We receive the inquiry when you send it. You can also contact us directly. Please leave payment card numbers and other sensitive information out of support messages.

How and why we use information

We use order information to process payment, fulfill and deliver purchases, manage returns and answer support requests. We use relevant records to reconcile transactions, investigate suspected fraud, secure our services, resolve disputes and meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on performance of a contract, compliance with law, our legitimate interests in secure business operations, or your consent when required.

Information shared to fulfill an order

Payment providers receive the details needed to authorize and settle payments. Fulfillment providers and carriers receive the delivery details needed to deliver a parcel or process a return. Hosting and customer support providers may process records needed to provide those services. We may also disclose relevant records to professional advisers, regulators or authorities when legally required or necessary to exercise or defend legal claims.

Website technology and storefront notices

This company website does not use advertising pixels or set advertising cookies. The hosting service may process connection data, such as an IP address, browser information and request timestamps, to deliver and secure the site. A store, checkout or payment provider may use other technologies. Review the notice and available cookie preferences at the storefront you visit.

International processing

Tessera Global LLC operates in the United States. Depending on your delivery address and the providers involved, your information may be processed in the United States and other countries. Where a transfer requires legal safeguards, we use the safeguards required by applicable law. You can ask us for information about the safeguards relevant to your order.

How long we keep records

Retention depends on the type of record and why we hold it. We keep order and payment-status records as needed to fulfill purchases, handle returns and disputes, and meet statutory recordkeeping obligations. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the inquiry and related follow-up. When those purposes end, we delete or anonymize information unless the law requires us to retain it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy of your information. You may withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent. Where available, you can appeal a decision on a privacy request or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to make a privacy request

Email support@globaltessera.com and describe the request. If it concerns a purchase, include an order number or the email address used at checkout. We may ask for enough information to verify your identity or an authorized representative’s authority. We will respond within the time required by the applicable law and explain any limits on what we can provide.

Policy updates and applicable law

We will post material changes to this policy with an updated effective date and provide additional notice where required. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, subject to applicable federal law and any mandatory privacy rights or protections in your jurisdiction.
